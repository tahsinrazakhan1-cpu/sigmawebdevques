Burj Khalifa

The Burj Khalifa is the tallest building in the world, located in Dubai, United Arab Emirates. It stands at 828 meters (2,717 feet) with 163 floors and was officially opened in 2010. Designed with a sleek modern structure inspired by the desert flower Hymenocallis, it is a symbol of engineering excellence and innovation. The tower includes luxury residences, offices, hotels, restaurants, and observation decks with breathtaking views of Dubai.
